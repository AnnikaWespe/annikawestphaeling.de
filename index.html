About

Hi, I'm Annika, originally I studied mathematics, soon found my way over to programming, first with JS/JQuery, then frontend frameworks, then backend development. I love learning new technologies, and I am capable of quickly adapting to new work environments.

Team collaboration is a part of my job that I enjoy immensely. On the operational level (learning and sharing new skills) but then also process of finding the most effective way through a multitude of requirements, open questions, and challenges and breaking them down into accomplishable steps. I’m also passionate about figuring out how to improve team communication, address unattended frustration or help to clarify roles and expectations.

With my quantitative background and my technical knowledge, Machine Learning/Data Science comes naturally as a next step. So far, I could polish up my statistical skills and Python programming, and work with libraries such as Pandas, Numpy and Matplotlib.
